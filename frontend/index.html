What is TeachAssist Dashboard?

          
        

        

          A free, student-made app for York Region (YRDSB) students that turns your
          TeachAssist marks into a clear, easy-to-read dashboard. See every assignment,
          test and culminating task, your Knowledge, Thinking, Communication and
          Application breakdown, and what you need on the next evaluation to hit your goal.
        

        

          Built for high school students and their families. Not sure yet? Explore the
          demo to try it with sample data, no login needed.
        

        

          This is a student-run website and is not affiliated with YRDSB. Your official
          marks are always on TeachAssist.
        

        

          
        

      

    

    
      

        

          
Help

          
        

        

          
How do I sign in?

          
Use the same student number and password you use on the YRDSB TeachAssist website. Type your number, tap Next, then enter your password.

          
Is my password safe?

          
Your password is sent securely to the sign-in service, and this browser keeps only a locked copy until you sign out. Sign out when using a shared phone or computer.

          
I forgot my password

          
This app can't reset it. Use the TeachAssist website or ask your school's office.

          
Sign-in keeps failing

          
TeachAssist is sometimes slow. Wait a minute and try again, or use Reset sign-in connection under the Sign in button. After several wrong passwords, sign-in pauses for a while to protect your account.

          
My marks look wrong or are missing

          
Marks come straight from TeachAssist. If a teacher hides a course mark, we estimate it from the posted work and label it Estimated. Your official marks are always on TeachAssist.

          
Refreshing takes a long time

          
Each refresh signs in to TeachAssist for you, which can take up to a minute when it's busy. Your last saved marks show while you wait.

          
Using a shared or school computer?

          
Tick This is a shared or school computer after you enter your number. Nothing stays on that computer once you close the tab.

          
Is this the official TeachAssist?

          
No. It's a free, student-run app that reads your TeachAssist marks. It isn't made by or connected to YRDSB.

        

        
Still stuck? Tell us what happened. Please don't include your password, or screenshots that show your student number or marks.

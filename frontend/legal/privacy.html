Privacy Policy

Last updated October 20, 2026

The short version
  • Your password is only used to sign in to TeachAssist for you. We never store it on our servers.
  • We don't sell your data. The site shows ads from Google AdSense, which uses cookies. Your marks and password are never given to advertisers.
  • You can delete your saved data any time by emailing us.

Who we are

This app is built and run by Intelligent Automations, an independent business in Markham, Ontario. It is not made by, or connected to, the York Region District School Board (YRDSB) or the TeachAssist Foundation. Contact: info@intelligentautomations.ca.

What happens when you sign in

You type your YRDSB student number and password. The app sends them over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our sign-in server, which passes them to TeachAssist (ta.yrdsb.ca) to fetch your marks, the same way your browser would. Our server does not save your password, and it is never written to our logs.

To keep you signed in, your student number and password are saved in this browser on your device until you sign out. Anyone using your unlocked device could open the app, so sign out on shared phones or computers.

What we store

  • On your device: your marks, settings, theme, dreams, goals, teacher notes and grade history. This stays in your browser.
  • Cloud backup: your dreams, goals and last-known marks are backed up so they survive a new phone. The backup is not labelled with your student number. It uses a one-way code made from it, so the stored data can't be traced back to you by itself.
  • Not stored: your password, your name from TeachAssist, or anything about your browsing.

Services we use

  • Cloudflare hosts the app and the sign-in server.
  • Supabase stores the cloud backup described above.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver fonts and the chart library. Like any website, they can see your IP address when these files load.
  • OpenAI powers the optional AI tutor in the Assistant. It receives only the messages you send to the tutor and its replies, plus your course names and current marks if you turn on "Share my marks". It never receives your password or student number. Questions about your marks, plans and school are answered on your device.
  • Google AdSense shows ads on this site. Google and its partners use cookies to show and measure ads, and may personalise ads based on your visits to this and other websites. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Ads and cookies

We don't use analytics. Ads are provided by Google AdSense, which places advertising cookies in your browser. We never send your password, student number or marks to Google or any advertiser.

You can turn off personalised ads in Google's Ad Settings, or opt out of personalised ads from many companies at aboutads.info. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Students under 18

The app is made for high school students. We only collect what's needed to show your marks, and we never sell your data. Ads on this site come from Google, which limits personalised ads for users it knows are under 18. If you're a parent or guardian with questions, email us.

Your choices

  • Sign out in Settings to remove your student number and password from this device. To also clear your saved marks and settings, clear this site's data in your browser settings.
  • Delete your cloud backup by emailing info@intelligentautomations.ca from any address with your request. We'll delete it within 30 days.
  • You can always use the official TeachAssist site instead.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. Big changes will be shown in the app.

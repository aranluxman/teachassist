Delete Your Data

Last updated October 26, 2026

The short version
  • In the app: Settings → Account & data → Delete my data. It's instant.
  • Can't sign in? Email us and we'll delete it within 30 days.
  • Your TeachAssist account itself is run by YRDSB and isn't affected.

Delete it yourself (fastest)

  1. Open TeachAssist Dashboard (the app, or ta.intelligentautomations.ca in any browser) and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account & data.
  3. Tap Delete my data and confirm.

This permanently deletes your cloud backup and everything the app saved on that device, then signs you out and cancels your saved login. If you used the app on other devices, open it there and sign out (or clear the site's data) to remove those device copies too.

What gets deleted

  • Cloud backup: your last saved marks, dreams, goals, and the first name you chose to enter.
  • On the device: your settings, saved marks and grade history, goals, profile, teacher notes and school choice.
  • Saved login: the locked copy of your sign-in is cancelled, so it can't be reused.

We never store your TeachAssist password on our servers, so there's no password to delete.

Can't sign in?

Email info@intelligentautomations.ca with the subject "TeachAssist Dashboard data deletion" and your student number. Your backup is stored under a one-way code made from your student number, so we need the number to find it. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm. We don't keep the number afterwards.

Signing in again

If you sign in again later, the app starts a fresh backup. Delete it again any time.
